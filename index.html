About Sarah

Where understanding meets meaningful change.

Hi, I’m Sarah.

I’ve always been fascinated by what makes us think, feel and behave the way we do. My background in psychology sparked that curiosity, which later led me to coaching and NLP.

For me, coaching is about creating a space where you can pause, feel heard and explore what’s really going on beneath the surface — without judgement or pressure.

Together, we can explore the thoughts, emotions and belief patterns that may be holding you back, gain a fresh perspective and work towards the changes that matter to you.
